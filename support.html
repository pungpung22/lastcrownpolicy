Contact support

Email pung0805@gmail.com for questions, bug reports, account and cloud-save issues, missing progression or currency, advertising rewards, purchase restoration, feedback, and feature requests. Support is available in 한국어, English, and 日本語.

Please include the app version, device model, OS version, language, affected screen or stage, approximate issue time, reproduction steps, and a screenshot when possible. For missing currency or progress, include the balance before and after the issue.

Security: Never send an Apple ID or Google password, full payment-card number, authentication code, private sign-in token, or an unredacted complete receipt.

Account and cloud-save help

  1. Confirm that the device has an active internet connection.
  2. Open Settings → Cloud Account and verify the connected Apple or Google account.
  3. Do not uninstall the app while a cloud-save issue is unresolved.
  4. Contact support with the last successful play time and the missing progress or currency amount.

Purchase restoration

  1. Use the same App Store or Google Play account used for the original purchase.
  2. Open Last Crown → Shop → Restore Purchases.
  3. Restart the app after restoration completes.
  4. If an eligible entitlement is still missing, email the product name and approximate purchase date.

Purchase restoration restores eligible store entitlements. It does not replace cloud-save synchronization of all gameplay progress.
